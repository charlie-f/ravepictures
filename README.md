# Bay Area Rave Pictures

Archive of `ravepictures.net`.

## News Update — 04/26/2026

Hey Everyone! I know it's been quite a while since I've updated the site — or basically done anything with it. Well, I've decided to fix some of the bugs and clean it up quite a lot. There was a mess in here from random things I was doing 20-some-odd years ago, and I opted to clean it up. There are also some good folders of history in here that I've left behind — those don't deserve to be removed.

I've published the complete site on GitHub for anyone to grab and do whatever they want with it, under the Apache 2.0 license.

🔗 [Ravepictures GitHub](https://github.com/charlie-f/ravepictures)

Life happens as expected. I know over time, some of us are no longer here. There are trinkets, memories, and a big time capsule in here. I'll keep the site running as best as I can — that's why I'm offering it to anyone who wants a copy. It's here in all its glory.

I hope things are good with you. Be well.

— Charlie
