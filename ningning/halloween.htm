This picture was taken about a week before Halloween, at the pumpkin patch in Morgan hill.
This is the pumpkin I wanted.
See the huge mound of pumpkins behind me? It's said that whoever sits there is pumpkin patch royalty.
This is the costume mom made for me for Halloween. She thinks I must have gone through a growth spurt while she was making it because by the time she finished, it was to small.
I didn't get to go trick or treating because mom said it was too cold. But I think the real reason why she didn't go was because she knew she would end up eating all my candy.
So this is what I spent my night doing: playing with plastic stars.
Do you see the wings on my back? What do you think of my antenae?
This is a view from behind, just so that everyone can get a better look. How come my mom never took my pacifier from me?
